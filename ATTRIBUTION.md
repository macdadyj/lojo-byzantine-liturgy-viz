# Icon attribution

These images are historical works from Wikimedia Commons. Each file below is marked Public domain or CC0 on Commons. They are stored in this repository at modest size for the teaching model. They are not photographs of a modern parish and they are not contemporary copyrighted icon prints.

Placement in the model, as you face the iconostas: Theotokos of Vladimir north of the Royal Doors, Sinai Christ to the south, St. Nicholas further north, St. John the Forerunner further south. The Royal Doors split the Ustyug Annunciation (Gabriel on the north leaf, the Theotokos on the south). The Mystical Supper is above the doors. Archangel Gabriel is on the north deacon door and Archangel Michael on the south. A Deesis panel and a feast tier (Nativity, Rublev’s Trinity, Transfiguration) stand higher. The dome medallion is the Daphni Pantocrator. The apse shows the Hagia Sophia Virgin and Child. Separate public-domain evangelist panels for the lower Royal Doors were not added; the Annunciation fills both leaves.
